Vieques Island Tours

Experience bioluminescent magic and wild horse encounters

Vieques: Island of Magic

Vieques is a small island off Puerto Rico's east coast known for its bioluminescent Mosquito Bay—one of the brightest bio bays in the world. You'll also encounter wild horses roaming pristine beaches and experience genuine Puerto Rican island culture.

Bio Bay Kayaking

Paddle through Mosquito Bay at night and witness millions of bioluminescent dinoflagellates create a magical light show with every paddle stroke. Tours operate year-round and accommodate all skill levels. Moonless nights offer the brightest displays.

Wild Horses & Beaches

Vieques is home to hundreds of wild horses. Day tours include beach visits, horse spotting, snorkeling, and local guide narration. Expect pristine beaches with few crowds and authentic Caribbean hospitality.

FAQ

What's the best time for bio bay tours?

Moonless nights (dark moon phase) offer the brightest displays. Tours operate year-round. Summer has calmer conditions.

How do I get to Vieques?

Ferry from Fajardo (45 mins, $4) or quick flight from San Juan. Many tours include transportation arrangements.

Is it safe to see the wild horses?

Yes. Local guides know safe viewing distances and horse behavior. Tours keep respectful distance and protect both visitors and animals.

What should I bring?

Swimsuit, towel, sunscreen, bug spray, light jacket (nights are cool). Tours typically provide all equipment.

How much does Vieques cost?

Day tours: $80-$120. Bio bay kayaking: $65-$95. Full-day combined tours: $130-$180 per person.
