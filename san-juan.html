Old San Juan

500 years of fortresses, cobblestones, and color

About San Juan

San Juan is Puerto Rico's capital and largest city, on the north coast. The two main areas for travelers are Old San Juan (the colonial walled city, with the cobblestone streets and the two big forts) and Condado/Isla Verde (the beach hotel strip, 15 minutes east). Most walking tours leave from the Plaza de Armas in Old San Juan and visit Castillo San Felipe del Morro (built 1539), Castillo San Cristóbal, and the Catedral de San Juan Bautista.

If you have one day, the standard pattern is a 2-hour Old San Juan walking tour in the morning, lunch on Calle Fortaleza or San Sebastián, and an afternoon at Condado Beach. Food tours are also strong — Spoon Food Tours and Flavors of San Juan both have years of solid bookings. Beyond the city, day-trips to El Yunque rainforest (40 min east) and the bioluminescent bay at Fajardo (1 hour east) are the most-booked external tours.

What to know before you book

  • Largest concentration of operators on the island — book 1+ week ahead for peak weeks
  • Old San Juan walking tours typically 2-3 hours, mostly flat with cobblestone streets
  • Castillo San Felipe del Morro and Castillo San Cristóbal are both UNESCO World Heritage
  • Condado/Isla Verde is the beach hotel strip — 15 min east of Old San Juan
  • El Yunque (rainforest) is a 40-min drive east, often combined with a Fajardo bio-bay night
  • Cruise port days: heaviest crowds; book private tours to skip lines
