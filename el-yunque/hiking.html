El Yunque Rainforest Tours

Hike to waterfalls and swim in natural mountain pools

El Yunque: Puerto Rico's Green Heart

El Yunque is a lush tropical rainforest featuring towering waterfalls, pristine pools, and incredible biodiversity. Tours range from easy walks to challenging hikes, accommodating all fitness levels. The forest canopy provides cool shade year-round.

Waterfall Adventures

Hike to El Yunque waterfall (75 feet), La Coca waterfall, and other cascades. Swim in natural pools fed by mountain water. Tours include swimming, photo stops, and local ecology education. Most hikes are 2-4 hours with moderate difficulty.

What to Bring

Water shoes or hiking boots with good grip, swimsuit, towel, sunscreen, insect repellent, and water bottle. Guides provide all necessary safety equipment. Bring $5-10 for snacks.
