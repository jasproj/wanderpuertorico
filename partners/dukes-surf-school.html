The school

Built on a board shaper's name

The About page names Eduard Dennis “Duke” Michael Alifier as owner and founder and describes him only in the past tense, with dates 1938 to 2008. He was born in Chicago, moved to Puerto Rico in 1965, opened a surf shop in San Juan and then Uku Surfboards in Rincón. The school says its mission centers on children and teens, particularly low-income youth and youth with autism. Its current owner and the founding year of the school are not published.

Tripadvisor reviewers name instructors Nico (most praised), Albert, Francis and Joey; none has a published title. The school says its instructors are ISA and USLA certified, which we have not independently verified.
