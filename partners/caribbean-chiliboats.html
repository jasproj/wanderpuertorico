The family

Waldy, Mariemma and José Alfonso

The About page names parents Waldy and Mariemma, with more than 30 years in sales and customer service, and son José Alfonso. It says the business began as a hobby in 2017, became a weekend project and is now the family's main business; they are also the official Caribbean sellers of Chiliboats. No formal titles are given. Reviewers praise Waldy as the guide most often.

The homepage shows DRNA authorization, a Puerto Rico Tourism Company endorsement and a Ports Authority authorization, which we have not independently checked. Across 144 Tripadvisor reviews we found no complaints; the caveats are that wildlife is not guaranteed and wind can shorten the cays route.

Before you go

Know before you go

Meeting points

San Juan tours leave from San Juan Bay Marina on Fernández Juncos Avenue. La Parguera tours leave from Punta Papayo, Lajas, about two hours from San Juan.

Arrive early

30 minutes before, with ID and the card you paid with, per the operator.

Bio bay rules

No swimming, and no lotions or bug spray on the water bike tour. Not recommended for claustrophobia or fear of the dark, per the operator.

Policies

Cancellation, weather and moon policies are not published; ask when booking. Guests over the weight limit cannot ride and are not refunded.

Questions

Good to know

What is a Chiliboat?

A two-seat pedal-powered water bike, more stable than a kayak, per the operator.

What does it cost?

$69 at La Parguera, $59 to $65 in Condado. Checked October 1, 2026.

Can kids ride?

From age 10 and 4 ft 6 in on the water bikes; from 5 on the boat tours.

Can I swim in the bio bay?

No, not on this tour.
