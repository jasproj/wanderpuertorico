The park

Nine lines, two rappels, rain or shine

The park is on Road 191 at PR-990 in Barrio Barcelona, Río Grande. Its FAQ says the lines are carbon fiber on a double-wire system with no hand braking, free lockers are provided, and tours leave at 9:30 AM, 12:30 PM and 3:00 PM. “We zip rain or shine,” the FAQ says; rain does not cancel, stop or delay a tour.

Owners are not named; Tripadvisor replies are signed JQmanagement, and no founding year is published. With more than 2,300 reviews at 4.9, the complaints that recur are that the lines are shorter or gentler than thrill-seekers want, waits between lines with big groups, and a narrow, steep access road.

    Owners are not named on the site. Site: junglequipr.com.
