The founders

Carlos Castro and Yolanda Ramos, marine biologists

The About page names co-founders Carlos Castro and Yolanda Ramos, both with coastal marine biology degrees from the University of Puerto Rico at Humacao and more than ten years in eco-adventure. The crew page lists Carlos (marine biologist, dive instructor), Yolanda (marine biologist) and Javier (marine biologist). No founding year is published.

The fleet is the company's own: Fish Tank, a 2015 Newton 51-foot power boat, and Ballyhoo, a 2018 Twin Vee 36-foot catamaran for private groups of up to six. Across 3,075 reviews the complaints are a small minority: thin snorkel instruction, dead coral at some sites and briefing gaps on urchins and reef contact.

Before you go

Know before you go

Check-in

Boat trips: the dive center at Roosevelt Roads Marina, Ceiba. Bio bay: Kayak Village, Las Croabas, Fajardo.

Limits

Ages 6+; under 12 shares a tandem with an adult on the bio bay; 240 lb per person and 400 lb per kayak, weighed on site. Pregnant guests cannot snorkel, per the listings.

Cancellation

By email only: by 6 PM two days before for 8 or fewer, seven days for 9 or more. No-shows and late arrivals are not refunded. Rain does not cancel; severe weather is rescheduled.

Fees

$3 DNR or DNER fee on the bio bay and Culebra tours, plus tax.
