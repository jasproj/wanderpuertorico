The boats

Catamaran, power catamaran and powerboat

Departures are from Villa Marina Yacht Harbor in Fajardo (the catamaran at Dock M-21, the motor vessel at Dock M-20). The site lists a 54-foot catamaran, a 36-foot motor vessel with 26-passenger capacity and a power catamaran. We could not reconcile an older listing that describes a 32-foot catamaran, so confirm the vessel when you book.

Owner and years in business are not published. Reviewers name Captain Kenny and Captain Ben and crew Lolo and Pablo. The recurring complaint in reviews is snorkel quality at some stops and guides who stay out of the water.

    Owners are not named on the site.
