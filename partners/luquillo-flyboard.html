The operator

A beach-side flyboard stand

The business operates behind the kioskos at Balneario La Monserrate in Luquillo, on the public beach. Its site lists a Luquillo address on PR-193; use GPS rather than Apple Maps, per Tripadvisor guidance.

The owner is not named on the pages we checked, and no founding year is published. Tripadvisor reviewers praise instructors named Jose and Yaddiel; neither title is published. The recurring negative reviews are about extra charges after booking and staff attitude, so confirm any add-ons and tips before you pay.

    Staff names are not published. Site: luquilloflyboard.com.

    Before you go

    Know before you go

    Requirements

    Minimum age 12, maximum weight 250 lb, and you should be comfortable in water, per the operator.

    Where

    Behind the kioskos at Balneario La Monserrate, Luquillo. Phone 787-909-1372.

    Weather

    Sessions can be cancelled for weather or mechanical problems; the Tripadvisor listing describes a reschedule or full refund. Confirm the policy at booking.

    Add-ons

    Confirm the price of photo and video packages and any tip expectations before paying.
