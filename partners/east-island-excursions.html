East Island Excursions

One of Fajardo's biggest boat-trip names. The operator says it has run since 1994 and describes a fleet of eight vessels plus eight mini boats from Marina Puerto del Rey. The standard trips are six hours to Culebra or Icacos with snorkeling, a beach, food and drinks; private sailing and power catamarans are sold by the boat.

The company

A fleet since 1994

The operator says it has run since 1994, calls itself the leading marine excursion provider in Puerto Rico, and says its vessels operate with approval from the US Coast Guard and Puerto Rico agencies. It publishes no owner, captain or staff names, so we list none. It also says it is a Blue Flag operator, an environmental program run by the Foundation for Environmental Education; we have not confirmed that independently.

Boats named on the site include the Zatara sailing catamaran, the Eco Isleño and Ocean Ray power catamarans and the Bellaventuras sailing catamaran. Per-boat specs are not published. Tripadvisor shows a 2026 Travelers' Choice badge.

    The operator publishes no staff list; it says its crews are bilingual and experienced. Site: eastislandpr.com.

    Before you go

    Know before you go

    Where to check in

    Marina Puerto del Rey, Fajardo; your confirmation names the dock. Phone and WhatsApp 787-409-2485.

    Included

    Snorkeling, beach, food and drinks on the day trips, per the operator; private packages differ by food level.

    Weather

    A third-party listing says a trip may be rescheduled or refunded if unsafe; the operator's own pages did not publish a policy we could read.

    Ages

    Culebra and Vieques snorkel trips from 4; Icacos sail from 3 per the booking page.

    Contact

    No email is published on the pages we read; use the phone or the contact form.
