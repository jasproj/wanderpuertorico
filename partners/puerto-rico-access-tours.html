Puerto Rico Access Tours

A Fajardo-based tour company that packages the east coast: a rainforest river walk in El Yunque, lunch at the Luquillo kiosks, and a night kayak into the bioluminescent lagoon, with round-trip transport from San Juan on most tours. The company says it is licensed by the Puerto Rico Tourism Company and holds an El Yunque special-use permit.

The operation

Permits, pickups and big groups

No owner is named on the site and no founding year is published; Tripadvisor reviews go back to at least 2018. Reviewers name guides AJ, Jaime, Talia, Emily and Carlos, without titles. Guides are described as bilingual and certified.

Group sizes run large: up to 20 on the rainforest tours and 30 on the bio bay, per the company. That is the most common complaint in reviews, along with weak glow outside the new moon, mosquitoes and a short rainforest walk. Two reads of the Tripadvisor listing on the same day showed 4.4 and 4.6, so we say about 4.5.

    Before you go

    Know before you go

    Pickup

    Round-trip transport from select San Juan and Isla Verde locations on the tours that include it; the meeting point is on José de Diego Avenue in San Juan.

    Ages and weight

    6 and up on the kayak tours; 240 lb per paddler. Adults with minors must show ID on the El Yunque combo.

    Cancellation

    Full refund with 24 hours' notice minus $10 per person; weather cancellations are refunded or rescheduled.

    Bring

    Repellent, sunscreen, a poncho and a waterproof pouch; none are included.
