Before you go

Know before you go

Meeting points

Melones Beach (reef) and Tamarindo Beach (turtles), per the operator. Some slots depart from other locations; check your time slot.

Requirements

Ages 7 and up for the snorkel tours; no swimming or snorkeling experience required for the kayak tour.

Cancellation

The Tripadvisor listing describes a full refund 24 hours or more ahead and a reschedule or refund for weather. Confirm at booking.

Price note

The operator's own site lists slightly higher prices than FareHarbor; the booking price applies.
