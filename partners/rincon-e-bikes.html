A new e-bike shop at Rincón Surf Resort, across from Sandy Beach, renting Himiway all-terrain fat-tire bikes by the hour or day and running a two-hour guided coastal cruise through downtown murals and along the Tres Palmas Marine Reserve. Riders 16 and up.

The shop

Founder John, fat tires, Rincón's coast road

The site names only “our founder, John,” and says the shop is “finally open” after years of logistics, so it reads as newly opened. Bikes are Himiway all-terrain fat-tire e-bikes with UL-certified Samsung batteries, per the site. Hours are Monday to Friday, 9 to 5.

There are no Tripadvisor reviews yet, so no rating is shown. Helmet and lock inclusions, weight limits and the cancellation policy are not published; ask when you book.
