Carabalí Rainforest Adventure Park

A working family ranch at the foot of El Yunque. The Diaz family bought Hacienda Carabalí in 1977 as a dairy farm and began renting horses on Luquillo beach in 1979. Today it sells rainforest and beach horseback rides, ATV and UTV tours, go-karts and a hayride with a petting zoo.

The ranch

Three generations on one hacienda

The park's history page says the Diaz family acquired Hacienda Carabalí in 1977, a dairy operation of about 90 cattle on 164 acres, and began renting horses on Luquillo beach in 1979; it describes three generations. The homepage says family-owned and operated and gives 600 acres, while the history page says the land grew from 164 to 510 acres, so we say only that it borders El Yunque. No owner names are published; Tripadvisor owner replies are signed by a manager, Alex D.

The park lists Tripadvisor Travelers' Choice, a Puerto Rico Tourism Company feature and a Hecho en PR certification; we have not verified these. It also runs a bar and grill and a gift shop. Reviewers name guides Andrew, Ricky, Victor and Ivan.

The one to book

2-Hour Rainforest Horseback Ride

The park's most popular ride, through the El Yunque foothills. Adults 12 and up $99, children 3 to 11 $74. Reviewers praise knowledgeable guides and well-cared-for horses; some note a slow pace, many stops and slow check-in.

Arrive early: reviewers report being asked to arrive about 45 minutes ahead.

Before you go

Know before you go

Where to check in

Road 3 Km 32.4, Exit 992, Street A, Luquillo. Phone 787-889-4954.

Ages

Rainforest horseback from 3 (adult rate 12+); beach ride from 8; ATV and UTV drivers 16 and up.

Weather and refunds

The park's refund and weather policy was not readable where we looked. One 2021 reviewer reports a no-reschedule outcome after a missed prepaid tour; confirm terms when you book.

Expect

Reviewers say ATV and UTV rides are slow and dusty, and that you will get dirty.

Contact

info@carabalirainforestpark.com.

Questions

Good to know

How far is it from El Yunque?

It borders the El Yunque foothills; the rides run through them.

What does a ride cost?

Horseback from $64; the 2-hour ride is $99. Checked October 1, 2026.

Can kids ride?

Yes: horseback from age 3; ATV and UTV drivers must be 16.

Is there a beach ride?

Yes: 2.5 hours, ages 8 and up, $140.

Who runs it?

The Diaz family, who bought the hacienda in 1977, per the park.
