The founders

Two directors, since 2007

The About page names Percy Rier and Dalberto Arce as founders and directors. It says the company began in 2007, won a Puerto Rico Tourism Company Quality and Excellence Award in 2008, added the Culebra Aquafari in 2009 and the mini boats in 2010. The homepage says “since 2001”; we go with the About page.

Guides are described as eco-interpretive, lifeguard and CPR certified. Reviewers name Noel, Jamil and Capt. Jim; the recurring complaints in 4,000-plus reviews are late guides, meeting-point confusion and leaky snorkel masks.

The one to book

Bio Bay Kayak Tour, Fajardo

Two hours after dark in Laguna Grande, including 15 minutes of instruction. Kayak, Coast Guard life jacket, navigation lights, bug repellent, water and granola bars are included, per the operator, with a guide and towing support.

Minimum age 6; kids 6 to 13 share a kayak with an adult, 14 to 17 need an adult on the tour. Weight limit 240 lb per person, 425 lb per tandem. No moon blackout: the operator says less moonlight means a brighter glow.

Before you go

Know before you go

Cancellation

Full refund up to 48 hours before; groups of 10 or more need two weeks. Inside 48 hours, late or no-show is fully charged. Weather calls may come on site the same day, with a reschedule, swap or refund. Self-cancelling for weather is still charged. Per the operator's FAQ.

Ferry delays

On the Aquafari, a delayed ferry means a reschedule or refund, per the tour page.

Questions

Good to know

Where do I meet?

Depends on the tour: Las Croabas for the bio bay, Sardinera for mini boats, the Ceiba ferry terminal for Culebra.

What does it cost?

Bio bay $70, Culebra $109, combo $165. Checked October 1, 2026.

Does the moon matter?

The operator runs year-round and says a darker night glows brighter.

Can kids come?

From age 6 on the bio bay and Culebra tours, 8 on the combo.
