The school

Four levels, from first launch to riding on your own

The school says it teaches in four levels: Into The Wind, Drift & Power, Chasing Wind and The Horizon. A beginner typically needs about 9 to 12 hours of instruction to ride independently, and every student receives an IKO certification to the level they reach.

Lessons are based at Ocean Park Beach and the school is affiliated with Numero Uno Beach House. Instructor names, the owner and the founding year are not published on the pages we checked.

    Instructor names are not published; the school says its instructors are IKO-certified. Site: kitepuertorico.com.

    Before you go

    Know before you go

    Season and wind

    The school says kiting season runs mid-November through August, with 16 to 25 knot trade winds and a 9 to 10 knot minimum for lessons. If conditions are unsuitable it may offer e-foil instead or reschedule.

    Requirements

    You need to be able to swim. Children are assessed on age, size, swimming ability and maturity.

    Group lessons

    The school's pages differ on whether lessons are private only or shared by up to two; the shared lesson is listed separately at $570.

    Cancellation

    Refund terms were not published on the pages we checked; see the booking page.
