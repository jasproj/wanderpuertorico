The founder

Moises “Moisty” Abreu, founder

The camp site names Moises “Moisty” Abreu as founder and describes him as an entrepreneur and former professional vert inline skater. It dates the Campamento VIP youth camp on Condado Lagoon to 2013; the company's own founding year is not published.

Reviewers name guides Alex, Carlos, Angel, Noel, Yazmin, Darien, Justin, Mike, Bryan, Jonathan and Jose, without titles. Before you go

Know before you go

Where

Ashford Avenue in Condado, across from the Condado Plaza Hotel. Reviewers say the shop takes some finding; look for the Flamboyan building.

Cancellation

Bike tour: full refund at 24+ hours, none inside 24 hours; weather calls get a new date or refund. Rental terms are on each booking page.

Ages

Bike tours 12 and up (the Old San Juan tour lists 12 to 68). Youth camp 7 to 16, life jackets required.

Before you book

Check your bike at pickup; brakes and tires are the most common complaint in reviews.
