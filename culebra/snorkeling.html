Culebra Snorkeling Tours

Explore pristine coral reefs and pristine waters with local guides

Why Culebra Snorkeling?

Culebra Island is home to some of the Caribbean's most pristine snorkeling spots. Crystal-clear waters, vibrant coral reefs, and abundant marine life make it one of the best spots for underwater exploration. The famous Flamingo Beach offers easy access to snorkeling just steps from shore.

What to Expect

Our Culebra snorkeling tours include all equipment (mask, fins, snorkel), experienced local guides, and transportation. Tours typically last 3-4 hours and visit multiple reef sites. You'll see tropical fish, sea turtles, rays, and vibrant coral gardens. All tours include snacks and water.

Best Time to Visit

Year-round conditions make Culebra snorkeling excellent. Summer offers calmer waters and better visibility. Winter provides slightly cooler water temperatures. Hurricane season (Sept-Nov) is less crowded with occasional discounts.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the tour?

Most Culebra snorkeling tours last 3-4 hours, including transportation, equipment setup, and 2-3 hours in the water at multiple reef sites.

What's included?

All tours include snorkeling equipment (mask, fins, snorkel), professional guide, transportation, snacks, and fresh water. Some tours include lunch for full-day options.

Do I need to be a strong swimmer?

No, you don't need to be a strong swimmer. Our guides teach beginner techniques and watch over all skill levels. Life jackets are available upon request.

What if the weather is bad?

Tours operate in most conditions but may be rescheduled for safety reasons. Free cancellation up to 24 hours in advance. Check with your operator.

How much does it cost?

Prices range from $55-$150 per person depending on group size and duration. Half-day tours average $75-$95. All-inclusive packages available.
