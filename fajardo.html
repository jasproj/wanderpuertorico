Fajardo

Marina town gateway to the islands

About Fajardo

Fajardo is the most-active charter port on Puerto Rico's east coast. It's about 1 hour from San Juan by car. Three reasons most travelers come here: the bioluminescent bay at Laguna Grande (one of the brightest in the world), Las Cabezas de San Juan nature reserve (a 316-acre headland with a 19th-century lighthouse), and the ferry terminal in Ceiba that runs to Culebra and Vieques.

The bio-bay tour is the headline experience. Most operators run kayak or pontoon trips that depart at sunset; the boats are timed so you reach the lagoon after dark, when the dinoflagellates light up most visibly. Tour quality varies — the smaller, all-electric kayak operators give a clearer experience than the larger pontoon boats, but they're more weather-dependent. New moon nights are best; full-moon weeks reduce visibility significantly.

Planning the bio-bay? See which Puerto Rico bay to book — Fajardo vs Vieques vs La Parguera → and how to get to Fajardo from San Juan →.

What to know before you book

  • Laguna Grande bio-bay is the headline draw — book 1-2 weeks ahead during peak weeks
  • New moon nights show the brightest bioluminescence; full-moon weeks are dim — see the 2026 new-moon booking windows →
  • Ceiba ferry terminal runs to Culebra (~45 min) and Vieques (~30 min)
  • Las Cabezas de San Juan reserve has a 19th-century lighthouse and 5 ecosystems in 316 acres
  • About 1 hour drive from San Juan
  • Best months: November-April; June-August has more rain and bigger crowds
