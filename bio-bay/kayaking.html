Bioluminescent Bay Kayaking

Paddle through glowing waters under the stars

Bioluminescence Magic

Bioluminescent bays contain millions of dinoflagellates that glow when disturbed. Paddle a kayak and watch the water light up with every stroke. It's one of nature's most magical experiences, and Puerto Rico has some of the brightest bays in the world.

Night Kayaking Adventure

Tours operate at night (usually 8 PM - 11 PM) when bioluminescence is visible. Moonless nights offer the brightest displays. Tours accommodate all skill levels and include tandem kayaks for those with less experience. Life jackets provided.

Best Time & Conditions

Tours operate year-round. Moonless nights (dark moon) create the brightest displays. Summer offers calm conditions. Winter can be windier. Rain doesn't affect the glow, but heavy storms may cause cancellations.
