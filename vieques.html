Vieques Tours

The least-developed of Puerto Rico's offshore islands — Mosquito Bay (the world's brightest bio-bay), wild horses, no tourist crush.

About Vieques

Vieques is a small island 8 miles off Puerto Rico's east coast, accessed by a 30-minute ferry from Ceiba (or a 25-minute flight). Half the island used to be a U.S. Navy bombing range and is now a National Wildlife Refuge — the protected ecosystem and the lack of large hotel development is what makes Vieques feel different from the main island. Wild horses roam the streets in Esperanza and Isabel II.

Mosquito Bay (Bahía Mosquito) on Vieques is, according to Guinness, the brightest bioluminescent bay in the world. The dinoflagellate density is several times higher than at Fajardo's bio-bay because of the protected mangrove ecosystem. Tour permits are tightly capped; only a small number of operators are licensed. Book ahead. New-moon nights are the brightest. The flip side: getting to Vieques takes a full day of logistics, so most travelers stay overnight in Esperanza.

What to know before you book

  • Mosquito Bay is officially the brightest bioluminescent bay in the world (Guinness)
  • Tour operator count is tightly capped by the National Wildlife Refuge — book 2+ weeks ahead
  • New-moon nights are the brightest; full-moon weeks are not worth the trip
  • Ferry from Ceiba (30 min) or flight from San Juan (25 min)
  • No major resorts — most travelers stay in small Esperanza guesthouses
  • Wild horses roam the streets — drive carefully, especially at dusk
