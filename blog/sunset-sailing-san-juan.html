Sunset Sailing in San Juan: Best Catamaran Tours & Romantic Experiences

Updated April 2026 · 9 min read

San Juan's north coast offers perfect sailing conditions at sunset—calm water, sky colors, and views of El Morro and Old San Juan skyline. Sunset sailing tours combine scenic beauty, refreshments, and relaxation on a boat.

Types of Sunset Sailing Tours

Standard Catamaran Sunset Tour

Large, stable catamaran with mixed-group setting. Beverages (beer, wine, soda), light snacks, live music or DJ. 2–3 hour duration. Good for social travelers and families. Cost: $65–85 per person

Romantic Couple's Sunset Sailing

Same catamaran with romantic upgrades: priority seating, champagne, special attention from crew, sometimes dinner. Perfect for anniversaries or proposals. Cost: $120–180 per couple

Private Boat Charter

Rent a sailboat or catamaran for a private group (2–8 people). Full control over route and timing. Captain included. Cost: $300–800 for 2–4 hours

What to Expect

  • Departure: Usually 3–4 PM, returning 6–7 PM
  • Duration: 2–4 hours total
  • Sailing route: North coast of San Juan with views of Old San Juan and El Morro
  • Food & drinks: Complimentary beer, wine, rum punch, soda; snacks like cheese, fruit
  • Entertainment: Live band, DJ, or recorded music

Romantic Elements

  • Book a romance package with champagne and dessert
  • Request special seating for sunset views
  • Time the tour for clear sunset forecasts
  • Bring a speaker for your own music (private charters)
  • Consider for engagement or anniversary celebrations

Practical Tips

  • Book in advance: Sunset sailing is popular; book 1 week ahead during peak season
  • Wear sunscreen: Reflected sun off water increases UV exposure
  • Bring a light jacket: Evening breezes can be cool on water
  • Arrive early: Boarding usually 30 minutes before departure

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a typical sunset sailing tour in San Juan? 2–4 hour tour departing late afternoon, sailing during sunset, with food, drinks, and music included.

How much does a sunset sailing tour cost? Catamaran tours: $65–100. Private boats: $300–600+. Luxury with champagne: $120–200.

Is sunset sailing good for romantic occasions? Excellent. Many operators offer romance packages with champagne, special seating, and attentive service.
