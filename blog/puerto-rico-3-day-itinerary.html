Puerto Rico 3-Day Itinerary: The Perfect Long Weekend (2026)

Three days isn't enough to see everything in Puerto Rico — but it's plenty to hit the highlights. This itinerary covers Old San Juan, a bioluminescent bay tour, El Yunque rainforest, and beach time, all based out of the San Juan area so you don't waste time on hotel changes. No passport needed, no currency exchange, and flights from the East Coast are 2.5–3.5 hours.

Before You Go

  • Where to stay: Old San Juan, Condado, or Isla Verde. All are in the San Juan metro, and everything in this itinerary is within 90 minutes.
  • Rental car: Optional. Tours include pickup, and Old San Juan is walkable. A car gives more flexibility for Day 2 and beach hopping.
  • Book ahead: Bio bay tours sell out — book at least a week in advance, especially for new moon dates. El Yunque doesn't require reservations for most trails.
  • Budget: $800–$1,500 per person for 3 days (flights + hotel + food + tours).

Day 1: Old San Juan & Bio Bay

Morning & Afternoon: Old San Juan

Start in Old San Juan — 500 years of Spanish colonial architecture packed into a walkable grid of colorful streets. Hit the big two forts: Castillo San Felipe del Morro (El Morro) is the iconic six-level fortress on the headland, and Castillo San Cristóbal is the largest Spanish-built fortification in the Americas. Both are National Historic Sites with great views.

Walk the blue cobblestone streets of Calle del Cristo and Calle Fortaleza. Stop for lunch at a local spot — mofongo (mashed plantains with garlic and pork) is the must-try dish. Grab a piña colada at Barrachina, where the drink was invented.

Browse the shops and galleries, check out the Paseo de la Princesa waterfront promenade, and watch for the famous stray cats that roam the old city.

Evening: Bioluminescent Bay Tour

This is the can't-miss experience. Head to Fajardo (about 45 minutes from San Juan) for a night kayaking tour of Laguna Grande. Every paddle stroke lights up electric blue — caused by millions of dinoflagellates in the water. Tours depart after sunset and last about 90 minutes.

Day 2: El Yunque Rainforest & Beach

Morning: El Yunque Rainforest

Get an early start and head to El Yunque National Forest — the only tropical rainforest in the U.S. National Forest System, about 45 minutes east of San Juan. Highlights:

  • La Mina Falls: The most popular waterfall — a 30-minute hike through lush forest to a swimmable pool at the base of the falls.
  • Juan Diego Falls: Less crowded, equally beautiful, with natural rock slides.
  • Yokahú Tower: A quick stop for panoramic views of the forest and coastline.

Wear shoes that can get wet, bring a towel, and expect brief rain showers — it's a rainforest. Most trails are moderate and well-maintained. You can explore independently or join a guided tour that includes pickup and waterfall access.

Afternoon: Luquillo Beach

After El Yunque, stop at Balneario de Luquillo — a beautiful public beach just 15 minutes from the rainforest. Calm water, palm-lined shore, and the famous Luquillo Kiosks right across the road: a strip of food stalls serving alcapurrias, bacalaítos, fresh coconut water, and cold beer. This is the best post-hike reward on the island.

Day 3: Beach Day or Adventure

Your last day — pick your style:

Option A: Beach & Relax

Spend the morning at Condado Beach or Ocean Park Beach — both walkable from San Juan hotels. Condado has a resort vibe with restaurants and bars nearby. Ocean Park is quieter, with a local feel. Finish with a long lunch in Condado or Santurce before your flight.

Option B: Snorkeling or Catamaran

Book a morning catamaran or snorkeling trip from Fajardo. Half-day trips visit Icacos Island or nearby cays — uninhabited islands with clear water and healthy reefs. Most include snorkeling gear, drinks, and lunch on the boat. You'll be back in time for an afternoon flight.

Option C: Food Tour

Join a food tour through Old San Juan or Santurce. Walking tours cover 5–8 stops — mofongo, lechón, local coffee, rum, and street food. A great way to close out the trip and discover spots you'd never find on your own.

Budget Breakdown

  • Flights: $150–$350 roundtrip (East Coast)
  • Hotel (3 nights): $450–$750 (mid-range in Condado/Old San Juan)
  • Bio bay tour: $55–$75 per person
  • El Yunque tour or self-guided: $0–$80 per person
  • Food (3 days): $120–$200 per person
  • Total per person: $800–$1,500

Tips

  • Bio bay timing: Book around the new moon for the brightest bioluminescence. See our moon phase guide.
  • Sunscreen: Use reef-safe sunscreen — it's the law in Puerto Rico and protects the coral.
  • Cash: Most places take cards, but keep $20–$40 for kiosks and small vendors.
  • Flight timing: Book an early arrival on Day 1 and a late departure on Day 3 to maximize time.

FAQ

Is 3 days enough for Puerto Rico?

Yes for the highlights — Old San Juan, bio bay, rainforest, and beaches. For Culebra, Vieques, or the west coast, plan 5+ days.

Where should I stay?

San Juan area — Old San Juan for history, Condado for resort access, Isla Verde for beach proximity. All three keep you central.

Do I need a rental car?

Optional. Tours include pickup, and Old San Juan is walkable. A car helps on Day 2 but isn't required.

How much does it cost?

$800–$1,500 per person for 3 days, including domestic flights, hotel, food, and tours.

What's the must-do activity?

Bioluminescent bay kayaking. Puerto Rico has three of the brightest bio bays on Earth — it's a bucket-list experience.
