Puerto Rico Catamaran Tours: Day Sails, Private Charters & Island Trips

Updated March 2026 · 9 min read

Catamarans are the predominant vessel type for day sailing and island excursions out of Fajardo. The twin-hull design gives them stability in the Puerto Rico Sound's light chop, deck space that monohulls can't match, and enough speed to reach Culebra in under an hour while providing a more comfortable platform than a powerboat for guests who aren't sailors. Most Fajardo-area catamaran tours sail to the nearby uninhabited cays—Icacos, Palomino, Palominito—for snorkeling and beach time, or run powercat-style vessels to Culebra for full-day excursions.

Puerto Rico has both sailing catamarans (using wind) and power catamarans (motor-powered, typically faster and less dependent on wind conditions). The sail-focused operators provide a more traditional sailing experience and tend to emphasize the journey as much as the destination; power catamarans prioritize efficient transit to the destination and more time at the islands.

Choosing Between a Sailing Catamaran and a Power Catamaran

The practical differences between sailing and power catamarans matter for planning:

  • Speed: Power catamarans reach destinations 2–3x faster. If you want to maximize time at Culebra rather than on the water in transit, a power catamaran is more efficient.
  • Wind dependence: Sailing catamarans need wind to sail; on calm days they motor most of the route anyway, making the sailing component minimal. Puerto Rico's trade winds blow consistently from the east, which is generally favorable for east-coast sailing.
  • Comfort: Catamarans of both types are more stable than monohull sailboats and larger powerboats in light-to-moderate conditions. Both handle the Puerto Rico Sound well on typical days.
  • Experience: If the sailing itself matters to you—the sound of the sails, the wind, the movement of a vessel under sail—book a sailing catamaran. If the destination matters more than how you get there, a power catamaran is more efficient.
  • Price: Power catamarans tend to cost more to operate due to fuel; private power catamaran charters typically cost more than equivalent sailing charters. Public tours are priced similarly regardless of propulsion type.
When to book a catamaran vs. a smaller powerboat: Catamarans carry more passengers and have more deck space than comparable powerboats. If you're booking as a group of 8–20 and want deck space to spread out, a catamaran is better. If you're a small group of 2–6 and want to reach distant destinations quickly, a smaller powerboat may be more economical and faster.
