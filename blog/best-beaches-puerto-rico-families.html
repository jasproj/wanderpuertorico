Best Beaches in Puerto Rico for Families

Updated April 2026 · 9 min read

Family Beach Essentials

The best family beaches in Puerto Rico combine calm, clear water, good facilities (restrooms, showers, shade), lifeguard presence, and activities for both young and older children.

Top Family Beaches

Condado Beach – San Juan

Best for: Families staying in San Juan, younger children. Protected, shallow, swimmable year-round. Lifeguards, restaurants, shops nearby.

Flamenco Beach – Culebra

Best for: Day trips, all ages. Consistently calm, clear water, excellent snorkeling from the beach.
