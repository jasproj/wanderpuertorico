Fajardo Boat Tours: Island Hopping, Snorkeling & Bio Bay from the East Coast

Updated March 2026 · 10 min read

Fajardo and the nearby town of Ceiba serve as Puerto Rico's primary launch point for ocean activities on the east coast. The Las Croabas marina in Fajardo hosts more tour operators than any comparable facility on the main island—sailboats, catamarans, powerboats, kayak tours, and dive charter vessels all stage from here. The protected waters of the Puerto Rico Sound lie directly to the east, with the populated cays of Icacos, Palomino, and Palominito close enough for a half-day trip, and the Spanish Virgin Islands of Culebra and Vieques reachable by fast boat in under an hour.

This guide covers the main boat tour options out of Fajardo and Ceiba, organized by tour type. For Culebra-specific information, see our Culebra Island Tours guide. For Vieques, see our Vieques Island Tours guide. For the bio bay specifically, see our bio bay tour comparison.

Snorkeling Boat Tours from Fajardo

The most heavily booked Fajardo boat tours are half-day and full-day snorkeling trips to the nearby cays. Icacos is the most commonly visited—it's an uninhabited island with a sand beach and reef on its eastern side. The water is clear, the reef fish are plentiful, and the cay is close enough that boats can reach it in 20–30 minutes from Las Croabas.

Full Day Snorkeling and Boat Experience (Pirate Snorkeling Shack)

Pirate Snorkeling Shack runs a full-day snorkeling and boat experience that covers multiple stops including reef and beach sections. Their base in Fajardo operates several vessels and runs both full-day and half-day formats. The full-day option covers more snorkel sites and includes more time at each location. They also offer jet ski rentals and boat rentals for guests who want to self-navigate.

Company: Pirate Snorkeling Shack · Location: Fajardo

Half Day Snorkeling and Boat Experience (Pirate Snorkeling Shack)

The half-day version covers a single snorkel site—typically Icacos or a similar cay—with time for swimming and snorkeling before returning to Fajardo. Half-day tours work well as a morning activity before an afternoon drive to El Yunque, or as an afternoon add-on if you've already done something else in the morning. The tradeoff: fewer sites and shorter time in the water than the full-day format.

Mini Boat Snorkel Adventure (Kayaking Puerto Rico)

Kayaking Puerto Rico offers a snorkeling adventure on a smaller, more maneuverable vessel—their "mini boat" format that lets you access shallower reef sections that larger catamarans can't reach. The smaller boat also means smaller groups, which creates a more focused snorkeling experience. The company has been operating Fajardo water tours for years and knows which reef sections have the best activity at different times of day.

Company: Kayaking Puerto Rico · Location: Fajardo

Fajardo Morning Snorkeling Tour (Sea Ventures Pro Dive Center)

Sea Ventures Pro Dive Center runs a morning snorkeling departure that checks in before 7:15am—early enough to reach the reef before most other boats. Morning snorkeling has consistently better visibility than afternoon sessions in most tropical reef environments because afternoon rain, boat traffic, and wind-driven turbulence haven't had time to reduce clarity. Sea Ventures also runs an afternoon snorkeling departure for guests who can't make the early start.

Company: Sea Ventures Pro Dive Center · Location: Fajardo

All Inclusive Beach & Snorkel Tour to Icacos Island (Caribe Bliss)

Caribe Bliss runs an all-inclusive snorkeling and beach tour to Icacos. Their all-inclusive pricing covers snorkeling gear, food and beverages on the boat, and transport—no additional fees on the water. Icacos is a small uninhabited island with a reef on its ocean-facing side and a calmer beach on the protected side. Caribe Bliss typically anchors on the beach side and leads snorkel excursions to the reef section.

Company: Caribe Bliss · Location: Fajardo

All-Inclusive 22ft Spider Boat to Icacos, Palomino & Palominito (Navea PR Adventure)

Navea PR Adventure operates a smaller 22-foot "Spider Boat" that covers three cays in a single trip: Icacos, Palomino, and Palominito. The multi-stop itinerary lets you compare the different character of each cay—Palomino and Palominito are closer to El Conquistador Resort and have different reef sections than Icacos. The smaller vessel handles the shallow beach areas at each island more easily than large catamarans.

Bio Bay Kayak Tours from Fajardo

Laguna Grande bioluminescent bay in Fajardo is the most accessible bio bay in Puerto Rico and the most heavily booked nighttime activity on the east coast. Tours involve kayaking through a narrow mangrove channel that opens into the main lagoon, where dinoflagellates create a blue glow in the water with every paddle stroke. See our full bio bay tour comparison for detailed operator information. Key operators from Fajardo include:

  • Kayaking Puerto Rico – Bio Bay Kayak Tour (one of the longest-running operators on Laguna Grande)
  • Island Kayaking Adventure – Glowing Bio Bay Kayak Adventure
  • Eco Action Tours – Bio Bay Night Kayaking Tour Laguna Grande
  • Puerto Rico Access Tours – Bioluminescent Bay and Bioluminescent Bay with Transportation from San Juan
  • Eco Adventures – Bio Bay Bioluminescent Kayaking Tour in Fajardo

Night Snorkeling and Evening Boat Tours

Nite Snorkeling and Boat Experience (Pirate Snorkeling Shack)

Pirate Snorkeling Shack runs a nighttime snorkeling trip that takes advantage of different marine activity patterns after dark. Many species that stay hidden during the day—octopus, lobster, moray eels, sleeping parrotfish—are visible at night with underwater torches. The experience is disorienting in the best way: the dark water lit only by flashlights creates a fundamentally different environment than daytime snorkeling at the same site.

Company: Pirate Snorkeling Shack · Location: Fajardo

Sunset Boat Experience (Pirate Snorkeling Shack)

The Sunset Boat Experience departs Fajardo in the late afternoon to catch the sun going down over the Puerto Rico Sound. The tour combines a boat ride out to the cays with swimming time and the sunset view before returning after dark. It's less intense than a bio bay tour but creates a different type of evening on the water—focused on the atmospheric quality of dusk on the ocean rather than a specific natural phenomenon.

Company: Pirate Snorkeling Shack · Location: Fajardo

Fajardo Reefs Morning Dive Excursion (Sea Ventures Pro Dive Center)

Sea Ventures runs certified dive excursions to the reefs around Fajardo's cays. The Fajardo reef system has moderate visibility, good fish density, and several sites appropriate for divers at different certification levels. The morning departure hits the reefs before afternoon boat traffic and potential weather deterioration. Sea Ventures is one of the established dive centers on the east coast; they also run dive trips to Culebra and Vieques for certified divers who want to reach more remote sites.

Getting to Fajardo from San Juan

Fajardo is about 45 minutes from San Juan via PR-3 east or PR-66 (the toll expressway, faster when available). The Las Croabas marina where most operators are based is at the northeastern tip of Fajardo—follow signs toward the marina once you're in the town. Parking is available at the marina lot.

For San Juan hotel guests without a car, some operators offer pickup service (check with each operator at booking), and rideshare apps cover the route, though the cost for a round trip can add up. The most practical approach for multi-activity east coast days is a rental car—it gives you flexibility to combine El Yunque in the morning with a Fajardo boat tour in the afternoon or evening.

Booking timing: Fajardo's most popular tours—especially bio bay kayaking and Culebra day trips—fill 1–3 weeks in advance during peak season (December–April). Off-peak (May–November), same-week booking is often possible. Morning snorkeling tours are less likely to sell out than bio bay tours, which have limited kayak capacity.
