Best Time to Visit Puerto Rico for Tours: Weather, Seasons & Activity Planning

Updated April 2026 · 10 min read

Puerto Rico has two main seasons: the dry winter (December–April) and the wet summer (May–November). The island's tropical location means warm temperatures year-round, but precipitation, water calmness, and crowd levels vary significantly by season.

Dry Season: December–April (Peak Season)

Weather: Sunny days, low rainfall, 75–85°F | Water: Calm and clear | Crowds: Peak tourism | Prices: Highest

Best for: Beach tourism, water sports, hiking, snorkeling

  • December–January: Highest crowds and prices (holidays)
  • February–March: Excellent weather, slightly fewer crowds
  • April: Transition month—still dry, warming up, smaller crowds

Wet Season: May–November (Off-Peak)

Weather: More rainfall, 80–90°F, higher humidity | Water: Can be choppier | Crowds: Fewer tourists | Prices: Much lower (30–50% discount)

Best for: Budget travelers, smaller group experiences

Hurricane Season: June–November

Direct hits are uncommon in Puerto Rico, but tropical storms are possible. Travel insurance with weather coverage recommended.

Activity-Specific Best Times

  • Bio Bay Tours: June–August (longest nights, darkest conditions)
  • Snorkeling & Water Sports: December–April (calmest water)
  • Hiking (El Yunque): January–March (less rain)
  • Sailing: December–April (predictable wind)

My Recommendation by Traveler Type

  • First-timers with flexible dates: February–March
  • Budget travelers: May–June or September–October
  • Families with school schedules: Christmas/New Year or spring break
  • Bio bay enthusiasts: July–August during new moon weeks

Frequently Asked Questions

What is hurricane season in Puerto Rico? June 1–November 30. Peak activity August–October. Direct hits rare but possible.

When is the best weather? December–April has most stable weather, lowest rainfall, calmest water.
