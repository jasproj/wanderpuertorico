El Yunque Hiking Guide: Best Trails, Waterfalls & Difficulty Levels

Updated April 2026 · 9 min read

El Yunque National Forest Overview

El Yunque is Puerto Rico's largest nature reserve and the only tropical rainforest in the U.S. National Forest System. Nearly 30,000 acres of protected jungle contain hundreds of trails ranging from easy 10-minute walks to challenging full-day adventures. The forest receives 100+ inches of rain annually, creating lush vegetation, waterfalls, streams, and misty mountain vistas. Elevation ranges from sea level to 3,500 feet at El Toro peak.

Best Trails by Difficulty

Easy Trails (30 minutes–1 hour)

Coqui Trail: Short, flat walk through subtropical forest.

Big Tree Trail: Loop walk among massive ceiba and native trees.

La Mina Lower Trail: Access to waterfall without steep elevation gain.

Moderate Trails (1.5–3 hours)

La Mina Falls: Popular 2-mile roundtrip to 35-foot waterfall and swimming hole.

Juan Diego Trail: 2-mile hike ending at an overlook with mountain views.

Challenging Trails (3–6+ hours)

El Toro Trail: Steep 5-mile hike to Puerto Rico's highest peak (3,500 ft).

Caimitillo Trail: Remote, less-crowded 4-mile trail through old-growth forest.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I know before visiting?

Check weather, book in advance during peak season, bring appropriate gear, and follow local guidelines for responsible tourism.
