Flamenco Beach Day Trip from San Juan: Ferry, Activities & What to Bring

Updated April 2026 · 10 min read

Flamenco Beach in Culebra is one of Puerto Rico's most beautiful and accessible day-trip destinations. Crystal-clear water, calm snorkeling conditions, white sand, and a laid-back vibe make it perfect for a beach day without an overnight stay.

How to Get There

Option 1: Self-Guided (Budget-Friendly)

  1. Rent a car in San Juan or take a taxi ($40–60)
  2. Drive to Fajardo (1 hour east)
  3. Park at the ferry terminal ($3–5 per day)
  4. Take the ferry to Culebra ($4–6, 30–45 min)
  5. Beach taxi from Culebra ferry terminal to Flamenco ($3–5)

Total cost (2 people): $60–100 plus ferry

Option 2: Guided Tour (Easier)

Book a full-day Culebra tour from San Juan that includes hotel pickup, ferry, snorkel guide, gear, and lunch. Cost: $85–150 per person

Your Day at Flamenco Beach

Timeline (Self-Guided)

  • 6:30 AM: Leave San Juan
  • 7:30 AM: Arrive Fajardo, park, breakfast
  • 9:30 AM: Ferry departs
  • 10:45 AM–4 PM: Beach time (snorkel, swim, lunch, relax)
  • 5:00 PM: Ferry departs Culebra
  • 7:15 PM: Back in San Juan

What to Do at Flamenco Beach

Snorkeling

The beach itself has excellent snorkeling. Rent gear on the beach ($15–20) or bring your own. Fish, coral, and clear water start 10 feet from shore.

Dining

Beach kiosks serve fresh seafood, sandwiches, and drinks. Limited selection; bring cash. Expect $12–18 for lunch.

What to Bring

  • Passport or government ID (required for ferry)
  • Cash (ATMs in Culebra, but limited)
  • Sunscreen (SPF 50+, reef-safe)
  • Water shoes (urchins, hot sand)
  • Snorkel gear (optional; rentals available)
  • Towel and change of clothes
  • Water bottle (stay hydrated)

Tips for a Great Day Trip

  • Book ferry tickets in advance: Peak season may sell out
  • Go early in the week: Wednesday–Friday are quieter
  • Leave early from San Juan: Early ferry arrival means more beach time
  • Bring snacks: Beach food is limited and pricey

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a Flamenco Beach day trip cost? Ferry: $4–6 each way. Snorkel gear: $15–30. Lunch: $10–20. Total: $40–70 per person.

Can I book a guided Flamenco Beach tour? Yes, many operators offer full-day tours with ferry, guide, gear, and lunch for $85–150.
