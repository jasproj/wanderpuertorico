Discover Puerto Rico's Best Snorkeling Destinations

Puerto Rico is one of the best snorkeling destinations in the Caribbean. With warm, crystal-clear Caribbean waters and vibrant coral reefs teeming with tropical fish, sea turtles, and other marine life, snorkeling here is an experience for all skill levels.

Our snorkeling tours take you to the best reef systems in the region. Whether you're exploring the pristine waters around Culebra, the bioluminescent-rich waters near Vieques, or the coral gardens of the mainland, each tour offers unique underwater adventures. Our professional guides provide all necessary equipment and expert instruction, making these tours perfect for both experienced snorkelers and curious beginners.

From morning reef tours to sunset snorkeling excursions, we offer a variety of snorkeling experiences tailored to your preferences and skill level. Most tours include transportation, snorkel gear, and time to explore multiple reef sites. Expect to see colorful parrotfish, sergeant majors, angelfish, and with some luck, manatees and sea turtles. Tours run year-round with excellent visibility most months.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I bring for snorkeling?

Most tours provide snorkel gear (mask, snorkel, fins) and life jackets. Just bring a swimsuit, towel, and sunscreen. We recommend wearing a rash guard or dive skin for sun protection. Flip-flops or water shoes are helpful for walking on rocky beaches.

Do I need snorkeling experience?

No! Our snorkeling tours accommodate all levels. First-time snorkelers are welcome. Guides provide instruction and can pair beginners with more experienced group members. We also offer slower-paced tours perfect for families and those new to snorkeling.

What marine life will I see?

Depending on location and season, you may see colorful tropical fish (parrotfish, surgeonfish, angelfish), sea turtles, rays, lobsters, octopuses, starfish, and diverse coral formations. Manatees are occasionally spotted in quieter areas. Each location has unique marine ecosystems.

What's the best time for snorkeling in Puerto Rico?

Snorkeling is excellent year-round, but the best visibility is typically May-August and September-November. Water temperatures range from 78-82°F throughout the year, making Puerto Rico ideal for snorkeling any time.
